# AI Usage Policy

This policy applies to all contributions to this repository, including code, especially PR content.

## 0. No vibe coding

Vibe coding — prompting an AI to "make it work" and shipping whatever comes out — is strictly prohibited. Every submission must be the product of your own understanding and deliberate work. Under the circumstance of a PR, if you cannot explain every line of your PR, it will be closed.

## 1. No AI-led work on mod content or game mechanics

Generative AI must not lead any work involving mod content, features, or modifications to game mechanics — including logic design, behavior changes, and gameplay-related implementations. Such work must be designed, written, and verified by a human. AI tools may only play a supporting, non-decision-making role (e.g. answering questions or reviewing code you have already written), and you remain fully responsible for verifying any suggestion against the actual game source code before using it.

## 2. AI allowed for mechanical tasks, with human review

You may use AI for:

- Adding or improving code comments and documentation.
- Reviewing code you have already written and looking for potential bugs.
- Mechanical, repetitive work. For example, bulk reformatting, generating code similar to existing patterns in this repository, and similar tasks.
- Algorithm design and optimization unrelated to game content — provided the design decision is yours. For example, if you already know an algorithm (e.g. DFS) is the right approach, you may hand the well-understood implementation to AI; but you must not let AI pick the approach for you and build on its suggestion.

Any AI-assisted output must be reviewed and confirmed by a human before submission. You are responsible for every line, regardless of how it was produced.

## 3. No AI bots as contributors

PRs authored or committed by AI agents/bots (including `Co-authored-by` trailers attributing AI) are prohibited.

Violations of this policy will result in the PR being closed without review; repeat offenders may be blocked.
